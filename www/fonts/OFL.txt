Montserrat
=========

The four .woff2 files beside this one are the Montserrat typeface, converted
from the upstream TrueType releases with no other change.

    Copyright 2011 The Montserrat Project Authors
    https://github.com/JulietaUla/Montserrat

Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1, which is the licence
recorded in each font's own name table (nameID 13) and reproduced with its FAQ
at http://scripts.sil.org/OFL.

The OFL asks that the font travel with its copyright notice and licence, which
is what this file is for: this gateway vendors every asset it serves, so the
fonts ship inside the wheel rather than being fetched, and nothing else in the
package would carry the notice.

It is named OFL.txt rather than LICENSE.md deliberately. The repository's
.gitignore denies *.md outside a short allow-list, to keep working notes out of
the tree - which silently swallowed this file when it was called LICENSE.md, so
the fonts were tracked and their notice was not. A test below the fonts now
holds the pair together.

Two conditions of that licence are worth stating plainly for anyone changing
these files:

* the fonts may be bundled, redistributed and sold with other software, but not
  sold on their own;
* a modified version may not use the reserved name "Montserrat".

Neither is a constraint on lmgateway, which is MIT and only redistributes the
fonts unchanged. Converting the format is explicitly allowed.
